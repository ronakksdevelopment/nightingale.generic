Frequently Asked Questions

What is the age criteria for Nursery admission?

A child must be at least 3 years old by 31st March of the academic session for admission to Nursery.

Is there an entrance test for admission?

For Nursery to Class 1, admission is based on age and seat availability. From Class 2 onward, a short written assessment is part of the process.

Does the school provide transport facilities?

Yes, we operate buses across several routes covering most parts of Imphal. Route details are available from the front office.

How can I pay the school fees?

Fees can be paid online, by bank transfer, or in person at the school's accounts office during working hours.
